Boarding

Enjoy your vacation or time away from home without worrying about your fur baby.

Dogs

Each dog kennel is 4×8 feet. Dogs are let out several times a day to do their business and play. There’s an indoor play area for when it’s too cold or wet outside.

The indoor play area is shared, so your dog needs to be dog friendly to use it while others are there.

Vaccines required: Rabies, Canine Distemper, Bordetella

Cats and ferrets

Cats get their own cat condo in the lobby. There are two condos, and each fits up to 2 large cats or 3 small cats who get along. A kennel in the dog area is available if absolutely needed.

Ferrets are housed the same as cats if no cage is brought.

Vaccines required for cats: Rabies, FVRCP, Feline Leukemia

Other pets

Birds, reptiles, rabbits and other small pets are welcome. Please bring their cage or enclosure (or a temporary one). They stay in the lobby or kennel area depending on space.

Vaccines: up to date where applicable

All pets must be up to date on vaccines for their species. It keeps everyone’s pets healthy, including yours.

About us

Little Village Pet Lodge is a pet boarding facility in rural Nebraska, just north of Funk. We take most any small pet, not just dogs and cats, and give your fur, feather or scaly babies a safe place to stay while you’re away.

Owner Ashleigh Jauken runs the lodge mostly by herself, usually with one or both of her young sons, Kaleb and Kolby, along. She has loved animals all her life, studied to be a vet tech at the Nebraska College of Technical Agriculture, and graduated with a degree in Applied Animal Science.

The name comes from the place. Funk is officially the Village of Funk, and with lots of different animals staying together, it’s a little village of its own. Being out in the country, “lodge” fit better than “boarding.”

Groomers: we’re looking for a groomer to rent space at the lodge. Call, text or email if you’re interested.
